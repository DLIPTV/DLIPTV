3. CONTENT SOURCING (Monthly Recurring)
================================================================================

Option A — Restream Providers (easiest):
  Buy restream access from established providers
  Typical cost: $300-800/month for full lineup
  Pros: Instant content, no maintenance
  Cons: Dependent on upstream, less margin

Option B — Direct Feeds + Own Sources (more control):
  Source channels directly via satellite/cable capture cards
  Combine with restream for hard-to-get channels
  Cost: $200-500/month for feeds + $500-1,000 hardware (one-time)
  Pros: Full control, higher margin
  Cons: Complex setup, maintenance heavy

Option C — Hybrid (recommended):
  Restream for bulk channels ($300-500/month)
  Direct feeds for premium sports ($200-400/month)
  Build VOD library from public sources (free)
  TOTAL CONTENT: $500-900/month

RECOMMENDED: Start with Option A, migrate to Option C at 500+ subs

5. PRICING & REVENUE MODEL
================================================================================

WHOLESALE TO RESELLERS (per credit/month):
  1 credit (1 month, 1 connection): $1.50 - $2.50
  Bulk discounts: 50+ credits = $1.25 | 100+ = $1.00

RETAIL DIRECT (end customers):
  1 Connection: $12/mo | $30/qtr | $54/6mo | $96/yr
  2 Connections: $18/mo | $45/qtr | $81/6mo | $144/yr

BREAK-EVEN POINT:
  At $2,000/month operating cost:
    - Need ~100 subscribers at $20/month average
    - OR ~50 subscribers at $40/month
    - OR 800 credits/month sold to resellers at $2.50 each

PROFIT PROJECTIONS:
  200 subs × $15 avg = $3,000/mo → $1,000/mo profit
  500 subs × $15 avg = $7,500/mo → $5,000/mo profit
  1,000 subs × $14 avg = $14,000/mo → $10,000/mo profit
  5,000 subs × $12 avg = $60,000/mo → $50,000/mo profit
