Setup Guide

Follow these steps to start watching IPTV on any device in under 5 minutes.

Android Phone iPhone Android TV Firestick PC / Web

Android Phone / Tablet

Works on Samsung, Xiaomi, Tecno, Infinix, and all Android devices.
1

Install an IPTV Player App

Open Google Play Store and download one of these free apps:

Recommended Apps

TiviMate IPTV Smarters Pro Televizo XCIPTV Player
2

Open the App & Add Your Playlist

After opening the app, choose "Add Playlist" or "Add User". You will be asked for:

Option A — M3U URL (Recommended) https://dl321dl.top/get.php?username=YOUR_USERNAME&password=YOUR_PASSWORD&type=m3u_plus&output=hls Option B — Xtream Codes API

Server: https://dl321dl.top
Username + Password from your DM

3

Wait for Channels to Load

The app will download the channel list. This takes 10-30 seconds. After that, you're ready to watch!

For the best experience, use TiviMate — it has the smoothest interface and supports EPG (TV Guide).

iPhone / iPad

Works on all iPhones running iOS 14 or newer.
1

Install an IPTV Player from the App Store

Open the App Store and search for one of these:

Recommended Apps

Smarters Player Lite IPTVX GSE SMART IPTV Flex IPTV
2

Add Your Playlist

Open the app, tap "Add Playlist" or the + button, and enter your M3U URL:

M3U Playlist URL https://dl321dl.top/get.php?username=YOUR_USERNAME&password=YOUR_PASSWORD&type=m3u_plus&output=hls
3

Start Streaming

The channel list loads automatically. Tap any channel to start watching. Some apps let you swipe left/right to change channels.

IPTVX has the best Apple TV-like experience on iPhone. Worth the few birr for the premium version.

Android TV / Google TV

Works on Sony, TCL, Hisense, Mi TV, Nokia, and all Android TV boxes.
1

Install TiviMate or IPTV Smarters

Open the Google Play Store on your TV. Search for and install:

Best for TV

TiviMate (Best for TV) IPTV Smarters Pro Televizo OTT Navigator
2

Add Your Playlist via Remote

Open the app, go to Settings → Playlists → Add Playlist, and type your M3U URL using the remote or use Xtream Codes:

Xtream Codes (Easier on TV remote)

Server URL: https://dl321dl.top
Username: YOUR_USERNAME
Password: YOUR_PASSWORD

3

Enable EPG (TV Guide)

In TiviMate, go to Settings → EPG → Assign EPG. This gives you a full TV guide showing what's playing now and next on every channel.

TiviMate Premium (one-time ~$10 via Play Store) unlocks the full TV guide, recording, and favorites. The free version works great too.

Amazon Firestick

Works on all Firestick models (Lite, 4K, 4K Max, Cube).
1

Enable Apps from Unknown Sources

Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options and turn ON "Apps from Unknown Sources" and "ADB Debugging".

2

Install Downloader App

Search for "Downloader" in the Amazon Appstore (orange icon). Install it. This lets you download apps from the web.

3

Download TiviMate or IPTV Smarters

Open Downloader and enter one of these URLs:

Download Links (type in Downloader)

TiviMate: tivimates.com Smarters: iptvsmarters.com/firestick

Download the APK file, then tap Install when prompted.

4

Add Your Playlist

Open the installed app, choose "Xtream Codes API" login and enter:

Xtream Codes Login

Server: https://dl321dl.top
Username: YOUR_USERNAME
Password: YOUR_PASSWORD

TiviMate on Firestick is the gold standard — fast, beautiful interface, full EPG support. Install it first.

PC / Laptop / Web Browser

Works on Windows, Mac, Linux — any computer with a modern browser. No download required.
1

Open Your Web Browser

Use Google Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari. Chrome works best for streaming.

2

Use a Web-Based IPTV Player

These players work directly in your browser — nothing to install:

Web Players (click to open)

Open Player HLS Player VLC Media Player
3

Paste Your M3U URL

Copy your M3U playlist link below, paste it into the web player, and click Load or Play:

M3U Playlist URL https://dl321dl.top/get.php?username=YOUR_USERNAME&password=YOUR_PASSWORD&type=m3u_plus&output=hls
4

Option: Desktop App (VLC Media Player)

For a better experience, download VLC Media Player (free) from videolan.org. Then:

Open VLC → Media → Open Network Stream
Paste your M3U URL → Click Play
VLC will load all channels. Press Ctrl+L to see the playlist.

The web player is the fastest way to test your account. Use VLC for daily watching — it's faster and has better quality controls.
